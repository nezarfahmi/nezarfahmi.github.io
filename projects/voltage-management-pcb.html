A four-layer voltage-management PCB for TOAD, a propulsive lander developed by Purdue Space Program Active Controls. The board converts a 36 V input into regulated 3.3 V, 5 V, 12 V, and 24 V rails using four DC-DC buck-converter stages.

Power monitoring and communication

The design integrates INA239 SPI power monitors for per-channel voltage and current telemetry, an STM32F405 microcontroller, and an MCP2562 CAN transceiver for communication with the vehicle.
